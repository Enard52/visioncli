# Code of conduct

We want VisionCLI to be a friendly project to contribute to, whoever you are and whatever your experience.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give constructive feedback about the work (not the person), and help newcomers find their way.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post sexual or violent content; publish others' private information; or keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

**Reporting:** if something makes you uncomfortable, email **visioncli@thethirdsourcers.com**. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
